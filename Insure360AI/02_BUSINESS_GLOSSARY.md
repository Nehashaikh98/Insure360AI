# Insure360 AI – Business Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Customer 360 | Unified customer-level view combining customer, policy, claims, complaints, payments and interaction intelligence. |
| Customer Segment | Customer business/value segment such as Premium or Standard. |
| Active Policy | Policy whose current status is ACTIVE. |
| Open Claim | Claim that remains unresolved, represented by an open/unresolved status. |
| Open Complaint | Complaint whose status is OPEN or IN_PROGRESS. |
| Overdue Payment | Premium payment with PAYMENT_STATUS = OVERDUE. |
| Interaction | Customer-service contact through CALL, EMAIL or CHAT. |
| Sentiment | AI-derived customer sentiment from an interaction transcript. |
| Cancellation Signal | AI-derived indication of cancellation/churn intent. |
| Urgency | AI-derived urgency level for an interaction. |
| Risk Score | Explainable customer risk score from 0–100. |
| High Risk | Risk score >= 60; immediate intervention recommended. |
| Medium Risk | Risk score 30–59; customer should be monitored closely. |
| Low Risk | Risk score < 30; customer currently considered healthy. |
| Next Best Action | Recommended business action based on customer risk and interaction/business signals. |
| Retention Call | Proactive outreach for a customer showing churn/cancellation risk. |
| Claim Escalation | Escalation of an unresolved/high-priority claim. |
| Cross Sell | Offer of an additional suitable product/policy. |
| Payment Reminder | Customer contact regarding overdue premium. |
| Service Recovery | Action to recover a customer relationship after a negative service experience. |
| Renewal Outreach | Contact for a customer approaching or passing policy renewal. |
| No Immediate Action | No immediate intervention required. |
| Premium at Risk | Premium belonging to HIGH-risk customers. |
| Customer Decision | Business-ready output combining Customer 360, AI signals, risk and Next Best Action. |
